2. OpenSLR 54, Large Nepali ASR training data set
   Used for: data/nepali_sim/ (NepDiar-Sim manifests, references, checksums), results/raw/nepali_sim*/
   Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
   Source: https://www.openslr.org/54/
   NepDiar-Sim is built from this speech, so every NepDiar-Sim file in this repo, and any audio
   you rebuild with scripts/make_nepali_sim.py, is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 too.
   Cite: O. Kjartansson, S. Sarin, K. Pipatsrisawat, M. Jansche and L. Ha, "Crowd-Sourced Speech
   Corpora for Javanese, Sundanese, Sinhala, Nepali, and Bangladeshi Bengali," in Proc. SLTU 2018,
   pp. 52-55. doi:10.21437/SLTU.2018-11

5. IndicVoices (Nepali)
   Used for: data/nepali_real/indicvoices/calls.txt (call ids), data/indicvoices_ne_calls/,
   results/raw/indicvoices_ne_calls/
   Licence: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/IndicVoices
   data/indicvoices_ne_calls/ holds two-party references we derived from the transcription
   segments (merged per side, speakers relabelled A and B, no speaker ids). They are shared under
   CC BY 4.0. scripts/real_nepali/prep_indicvoices.py rebuilds them from the official release.
   Cite: T. Javed et al., "IndicVoices: Towards building an Inclusive Multilingual Speech Dataset
   for Indian Languages," in Findings of ACL 2024, pp. 10740-10782. doi:10.18653/v1/2024.findings-acl.639

6. LibriSpeech (dev-clean and test-clean)
   Used for: data/english_sim/ (English twin manifests, references, checksums), results/raw/english_sim*/
   Licence: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   Source: https://www.openslr.org/12/
   Cite: V. Panayotov, G. Chen, D. Povey and S. Khudanpur, "LibriSpeech: an ASR corpus based on
   public domain audio books," in Proc. ICASSP 2015, pp. 5206-5210. doi:10.1109/ICASSP.2015.7178964
   The English twin reuses NepDiar-Sim's room responses, noise plan and turn timing, so the twin
   files in this repo, and any audio you rebuild with scripts/make_english_sim.py, are shared
   under CC BY-SA 4.0 like NepDiar-Sim. The LibriSpeech speech inside them stays CC BY 4.0.
